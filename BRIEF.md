# Personal Interactive Timeline of Human History (PC) - brief from the user
Scope: Interactive timeline from the Big Bang to the present, for PC. Aim to include everything, from major events to obscure, nearly forgotten details.
Myths & Legends: All myths and legends from all world cultures, placed alongside the historical events they relate to (or are claimed to), presented without preference.
Core principle: Completely non-biased presentation, neutral even relative to the user's own views. AI is preferred to help keep impartiality.
Core focus: Present alternative views and interpretations of key events and mysteries alongside mainstream ones, never defaulting to one narrative.
Starting topics of personal interest (not limits): Younger Dryas (incl. massive flood interpretations linked to global myths); Gobekli Tepe; Pyramids; dinosaur/dragon mythology connections; Biblical flood stories; Book of Enoch; potential suppression/control of historical knowledge by priests, kings, authorities.
Navigation: How the user moves through and explores the timeline is important and needs careful design.
Purpose: To "see the picture" and resolve personal questions about these events and alternative interpretations.
Long-term: Potentially lifelong; may be open-sourced later for collaborative contributions.
Status: Early planning / concept stage.
